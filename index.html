Brickfall

Quest 3 · hands only

Virtual toy bricks and balls in your real room. Throw, knock over, rebuild.

In the headset

  1. Pinch or make a fist in empty air and a ball appears in your hand. Swing and open your hand to throw.
  2. Pinch or grab a brick or a ball to pick it up. Your hands also push things.
  3. Turn your palm toward your face to open the menu. Tap buttons with the other index finger.
  4. Floor, walls and tables from your Space Setup are solid.

Preview on this screen: drag to look around, click to throw, R rebuild, P pile type, B ball type, G gravity, S slow motion, C clear balls.
